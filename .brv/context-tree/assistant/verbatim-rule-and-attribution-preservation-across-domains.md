# Verbatim Rule and Attribution Preservation Across Domains

Multiple domains enforce strict preservation of operational rules, lists, and attribution metadata, requiring that all such knowledge be captured exactly as authored to support traceability, audit, and process rigor.

## Evidence

- **assistant**: Guidelines require verbatim retention of rules, patterns, lists, and author/timestamp metadata; synthesis entries confirm this standard applies cross-domain.
- **facts**: Conventions and guardrail protocols explicitly document and preserve process lists, credential requirements, and operational rules, mirroring assistant domain standards.
- **project_management**: Review process and retrospectives document all process steps and action items with explicit attribution, adhering to cross-domain rule preservation.
