# Postora Business Profile & Knowledge Base

## 1. Company Overview
- **Name:** Postora
- **Platform URL:** [postora.cloud](https://postora.cloud)
- **Tagline:** The Ultimate AI-Powered Social Media Management Platform.
- **Description:** Postora is a comprehensive social media management platform designed for individuals, creators, and agencies to connect, manage, and automate their social media presence. It supports multiple platforms from a unified dashboard, enabling seamless scheduling, AI-assisted content generation, and detailed analytics.

## 2. Core Products & Services
1. **Multi-Platform Management:** Connect and manage accounts across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X (Twitter), and Pinterest.
2. **Post Scheduling & Publishing:** Create posts, upload media, and schedule them to be published automatically at specific times.
3. **AI Content Generation:**
   - **AI Captions:** Generate engaging text tailored to the selected platform.
   - **AI Hashtags:** Automatically suggest trending and relevant hashtags.
   - **AI Image Generation:** Create unique images based on text prompts directly inside the app.
4. **Media Library:** Upload, store, and manage images and videos (powered by Supabase Storage) to use in posts.
5. **Automation & Workflows:** (Powered by n8n) Automated event triggers like responding to messages and managing webhooks (e.g., Instagram DMs).
6. **Analytics (Coming/Available depending on plan):** View performance metrics for connected social accounts.

## 3. Supported Platforms & Capabilities
- **Facebook:** Pages and Groups posting.
- **Instagram:** Direct publishing to Instagram Business/Creator accounts. Supports Reels, posts, and Stories. Direct Message automation via webhooks.
- **TikTok:** Video publishing with built-in UX compliance (commercial content disclosure, duet/stitch toggles, privacy settings).
- **YouTube:** Video uploading and scheduling to specific channels.
- **Pinterest:** Pin creation on selected boards.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
**Q: How do I connect my Instagram account?**
A: Go to the "Profiles" tab in your dashboard, click "Add Account," select Instagram, and follow the Meta OAuth login process. Ensure your Instagram account is a Professional/Business account linked to a Facebook Page.

**Q: My scheduled post failed. Why?**
A: Posts can fail for a few reasons: an expired OAuth token, media size/format not supported by the platform (e.g., video too long for TikTok), or missing permissions. Please check the "History" tab for the specific error message, or try reconnecting your account to refresh the token.

**Q: Can I use Postora on my mobile phone?**
A: Yes, Postora is a fully responsive web application that works seamlessly on mobile browsers.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Absolutely. Postora uses industry-standard encryption, OAuth 2.0 for social platform connections (meaning we never see your passwords), and robust database security (powered by Supabase). We also support Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for your Postora account.

**Q: What is the "Understand Media" feature?**
A: This feature allows Postora's AI to process and understand voice notes, images, and documents received through integrated channels like WhatsApp and Instagram DMs, enabling smarter automated replies.
