# Postora Product Marketing Context

Use `docs/POSTORA-AGENT-CONTEXT.md` as the detailed source of truth for Postora's product capabilities, platform coverage, positioning, pricing, competitor analysis, marketing plan, and operational context.

## Product

Postora is an AI-native social media operations platform for creators, agencies, SMBs, and lean marketing teams. It combines multi-platform publishing, scheduling, AI content creation, analytics, a Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp inbox, Cloudinary media management, and automation through a public API, n8n node, Make.com, and MCP.

## Positioning

Primary message: **One social command center. No channel tax. No legacy bloat.**

The core differentiation is a unified workflow: create, publish, schedule, analyze, respond, and automate from one product, instead of buying separate scheduling, inbox, AI, analytics, and automation tools.

## ICP

- Creator-operators managing multiple social accounts
- Lean agencies managing client accounts and repeatable workflows
- SMB marketing teams needing publishing, messaging, and analytics without enterprise pricing

## Primary Marketing Angles

- Eleven platform integrations in one dashboard
- AI across content creation, media, intelligence, and replies
- Unified Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp inbox
- Public API and n8n node for automation-first teams
- Early-bird Pro pricing currently advertised at $5/month; verify current pricing before publication
